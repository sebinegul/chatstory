RELATIONSHIP: GROUP

GOAL

Tell the story of a group of people who shared memories together.

The book should feel like reliving the group's journey, capturing its
energy, humor, milestones, and friendships.

Avoid focusing on only one or two people unless they naturally dominate
important events.

The narration should celebrate the collective memories of the group.

--------------------------------------------------------

AI SHOULD LOOK FOR

--------------------------------------------------------
1. The Group Begins

Look for:
- earliest messages
- welcome messages
- group creation
- introductions
- first conversations

Purpose:
Introduce how the group came together.

--------------------------------------------------------
2. Finding Our Rhythm

Look for:
- regular chatting
- increasing activity
- everyone getting comfortable
- recurring participants

Purpose:
Show how strangers became a community.

--------------------------------------------------------
3. Endless Conversations

Look for:
- longest conversations
- late-night discussions
- random topics
- everyday chatting

Purpose:
Highlight how the group became part of daily life.

--------------------------------------------------------
4. Inside Jokes

Look for:
- repeated jokes
- funny nicknames
- memes
- GIF discussions
- emoji spam
- recurring phrases

Purpose:
Capture the humor unique to the group.

--------------------------------------------------------
5. Celebrations

Look for:
- birthdays
- anniversaries
- congratulations
- promotions
- weddings
- achievements
- festivals

Purpose:
Celebrate happy milestones together.

--------------------------------------------------------
6. Adventures Together

Look for:
- trips
- outings
- meetups
- college events
- office parties
- vacations
- photos being shared
- planning discussions

Purpose:
Tell the story of shared experiences beyond the chat.

--------------------------------------------------------
7. Helping Each Other

Look for:
- emotional support
- advice
- helping someone
- career guidance
- exam preparation
- financial help
- family support

Purpose:
Show the strength of the community.

--------------------------------------------------------
8. Big Moments

Look for:
- major announcements
- engagements
- babies
- new jobs
- graduations
- moving abroad
- important life updates

Purpose:
Highlight life-changing moments witnessed together.

--------------------------------------------------------
9. Quiet Days

Look for:
- long silence
- inactive periods
- group becoming quiet
- return after months

Purpose:
Show that even quiet groups come alive again.

--------------------------------------------------------
10. The Group Never Changed

Look for:
- recent conversations
- old jokes returning
- same personalities
- familiar conversations

Purpose:
Show that the bond still exists despite time.

--------------------------------------------------------
11. Our Favorite Moments

AI should choose the strongest emotional and funniest conversations
throughout the group's history.

Do not simply choose the longest conversations.

--------------------------------------------------------
12. A Story Still Being Written

End with an emotional closing about friendships, memories, and the
journey continuing.

--------------------------------------------------------

AI SHOULD ANALYZE

- Most active members
- Message bursts
- Longest conversations
- Emoji usage
- Shared laughter
- Shared achievements
- Trips
- Celebrations
- Arguments that were resolved
- Polls and planning
- Frequently used phrases
- Recurring jokes
- Group traditions
- Message streaks
- Long inactive periods
- Most memorable days

--------------------------------------------------------

IMPORTANT RULES

1. Never invent conversations.

2. Never invent events.

3. Every chapter must be supported by real messages.

4. Skip chapters with weak evidence.

5. Do not force all chapters.

6. Focus on the group as a whole, not one individual.

7. Humor should feel natural.

8. Narration should connect memories instead of summarizing messages.

9. Use verbatim quotes only.

10. End every chapter with a feeling, not just a summary.
