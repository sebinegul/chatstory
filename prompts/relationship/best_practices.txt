GENERAL AI RULES

The AI must NOT simply summarize the chat.

Instead it should identify STORY ARCS.

For every chapter:

1. Search the entire chat.
2. Collect 10-100 relevant messages.
3. Score them based on:
   - emotion
   - uniqueness
   - conversation length
   - replies
   - emoji density
   - milestones
4. Choose only the strongest moments.
5. Write narration around REAL messages.
6. Never invent facts.
7. Never force chronology.
8. Skip chapters with weak evidence.
9. Prefer quality over quantity.
10. Every chapter should answer:
   "Why does this memory matter?"

Narration should connect memories instead of describing every message.

The book should read like a memoir, not a WhatsApp export.
