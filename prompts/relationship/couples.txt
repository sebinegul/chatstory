RELATIONSHIP: COUPLES

GOAL
Tell the story of how two people slowly became each other's home.
The narration should feel like reading a love story, not a timeline.

AI SHOULD LOOK FOR

1. First Conversation
- first messages
- awkward introductions
- greetings
- ice breakers

2. Becoming Comfortable
- inside jokes
- nicknames
- daily texting begins
- long conversations

3. First "Care"
Look for:
- "eat"
- "reached?"
- "sleep"
- "take care"
- checking in
- concern

4. The Happy Days
Look for:
- message bursts
- laughing
- emojis
- teasing
- memes
- flirting
- playful fights

5. Our Little Traditions
Look for:
- daily routines
- morning/night wishes
- recurring phrases
- pet names

6. The First Big Moment
Look for:
- proposal
- confession
- first "I love you"
- anniversary
- engagement
- first date

7. Distance & Missing Each Other
Look for:
- "miss you"
- "come soon"
- travel
- silence
- longing

8. The Fights
Look for:
- arguments
- misunderstandings
- apologies
- "sorry"
- emotional conversations

9. Choosing Each Other Again
Look for:
- reconciliations
- forgiveness
- promises
- reassurance

10. Dreams Together
Look for:
- marriage
- house
- children
- travel
- future plans

11. Our Favourite Memories
AI picks the happiest conversations with highest emotional score.

12. What Never Changed
Summarize recurring habits, jokes, love, support.

IMPORTANT
Never force all chapters.
Only generate chapters that genuinely exist.
Skip empty chapters.
