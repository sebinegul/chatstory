RELATIONSHIP: TRIBUTE / MEMORIAL

GOAL
Create a careful remembrance book from the messages that remain.

The narration should feel gentle, reverent, and never sensational.
Prefer short sentences. Soft tone. No dramatic speeches.

AI SHOULD LOOK FOR

1. Ordinary care
- check-ins
- shared plans
- small kindnesses
- everyday affection

2. Moments of support
- comfort
- apologies
- encouragement
- being present

3. What still remains
- lines that feel personal
- nicknames
- habits unique to them

RULES
- Never invent last words
- Never invent heaven, angels, or afterlife speeches
- Never invent illness, death details, or funerals the chat does not contain
- Do not exaggerate grief
- Honor only what the messages still know
- Keep chapters brief when evidence is thin
