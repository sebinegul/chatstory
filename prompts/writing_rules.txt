Writing Rules

Use elegant, simple English.

Short paragraphs.

Avoid repetitive wording.

Avoid clichés.

Avoid "their journey continued..."

Avoid "through thick and thin."

Avoid AI phrases like:
"It was clear that..."
"This moment marked..."
"As time went on..."

Show instead of tell.

Let real quotes carry emotion.

Never exaggerate.

Never over-dramatize.

Use emojis only inside quoted messages.

Never use emojis in narration.

Write naturally.

The narration should disappear behind the memories.
